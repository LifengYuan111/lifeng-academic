## Stormwater fate and transport of biological contaminants (AnCOR)

**Funded by U.S. EPA Homeland Security Research Program, 2021–2024**  
EPA, U.S. Coast Guard, and DHS

Led high-resolution SWMM development to simulate *Bacillus anthracis* transport across various urban surfaces under multiple storm scenarios; developed custom Python scripts and integrated field data for decontamination-strategy evaluation.

**Tools:** SWMM/PCSWMM · Python/IronPython · GIS

**Technical components:** Urban catchment modeling · Rainfall-scenario design · Contaminant fate and transport · Model automation

**Role:** Model development · Python scripting · Field-data integration · Scenario evaluation

## Climate-sensitive agricultural management and erosion prediction

**WEPP Modeling · Funded by USDA-ARS, 2020–2021**  
Grazinglands Research Lab, El Reno, Oklahoma

Simulated impacts of storm intensification and climate change on crop yield and soil loss under multiple tillage systems using WEPP and Python automation.

**Tools:** WEPP · Python · Climate-model ensembles · CLIGEN

**Technical components:** Climate-data processing · Batch simulation · Cropping and tillage scenarios · Runoff, soil-loss, and yield assessment

**Role:** Model development · Workflow automation · Data analysis · Visualization and manuscript preparation

## Advancing streamflow prediction with SWAT and machine learning

**Funded by U.S. EPA, 2017–2020**  
Robert S. Kerr Environmental Research Center, Ada, Oklahoma

Developed hybrid SWAT-SVR and SWAT-Wavelet models to improve streamflow and sediment predictions; supported EPA’s watershed and water-quality tool-evaluation efforts.

**Tools:** SWAT/SWAT-CUP · Support vector regression · Discrete wavelet transform · GIS

**Technical components:** Hydrologic calibration · Machine-learning integration · Multi-site evaluation · Performance visualization

**Role:** Model development · Data processing · Machine-learning integration · Statistical evaluation and manuscript preparation

## Soil erosion and GIS-based risk mapping in the Loess Plateau

**Funded by NSFC, Grant No. 41001158, 2011–2013**  
Chinese Academy of Sciences

Principal Investigator of a rill-erosion modeling project using 3D Cellular Automata and GIS; validated with laboratory and field rainfall experiments and 3D laser scanning.

**Tools:** Cellular Automata · GIS · 3D laser scanning · Rainfall simulation

**Technical components:** Rill-erosion process modeling · Experimental validation · Terrain analysis

**Role:** Principal Investigator · Model development · Experimental design · Analysis and reporting

**Role:** Framework development · GIS processing · Risk assessment · Database development

## Climate–hydrology–substance transport

**Poyang Lake Watershed · Funded by CAS Knowledge Innovation Program, 2009–2011**

Used geostatistics and wavelet analysis to simulate long-term runoff, rainfall variability, and sediment transport in the Poyang Lake basin.

**Tools:** Geostatistics · Wavelet analysis · GIS · Hydrologic data analysis

**Technical components:** Long-term rainfall and runoff variability · Sediment-transport assessment · Spatial analysis

**Role:** Data processing · Statistical analysis · Hydrologic interpretation and reporting
